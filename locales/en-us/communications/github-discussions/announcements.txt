Welcome to Health Economics Guide Discussions

Health Economics Guide now has GitHub Discussions, a place to ask questions, suggest ideas, and share what you have done with it.

Health Economics Guide is a practical handbook of best practices for health economics, covering tax-funded, social-insurance, private-insurance, and mixed systems worldwide. It is written for chief executives and senior leaders, directors of digital and transformation, product and programme leads, clinical and care professionals, and operational managers.

## What this means for you

- You can ask questions and get answers from other readers.
- You can suggest topics, fixes, and improvements before anyone writes them.
- You can share how you use the guide.
