Say hello and tell us how you use Health Economics Guide

Welcome to the Health Economics Guide Discussions. This is the place for introductions, opinions, and open conversation about the guide.

I am a maintainer of the project. I wrote Health Economics Guide because I wanted a practical handbook of best practices for health economics, covering tax-funded, social-insurance, private-insurance, and mixed systems worldwide to be easy to find and use. I would like to hear who is reading it and why.

**One question to start:** Which health economics idea do you use most often in your work, and where did you learn it?

A sentence or two is plenty. Replies to other people are welcome too.

## Where other topics belong

- Suggestions for new or changed topics: Ideas
- Errors or broken links: Issues (https://github.com/health-economics-guide/health-economics-guide/issues)
