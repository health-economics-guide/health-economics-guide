Summary: Health Economics Guide is a handbook for professionals in health and care. The handbook covers foundations, evaluation, health systems, policy, and digital health. It serves executives, policy leaders, clinicians, and program managers.

Benefits: This provides clear consistent framework ideas for complex decisions. Find plain language for concepts that usually hide behind jargon. Follow a source you can actually verify, not a vague claim repeated from memory.

Why this matters: Health spending decisions shape who gets timely care and who waits. They shape who is covered and who pays out of pocket. Leaders who understand the economics behind those decisions make fairer, more defensible calls. They can explain those calls clearly to a board, a clinician, or the public. This guide exists so that understanding is not locked behind an economics degree or a single country's system.
